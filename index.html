How it works

Live in three steps

No migration project, no vendor onboarding. Bring your keys and your team — the rest is open protocol.

  1. 01

    Create your identity

    Generate a keypair on your device. You control it — and you are responsible for backing it up.

  2. 02

    Connect a relay

    Use relay.bitos.space or run your own. Your workspace lives on infrastructure you control.

  3. 03

    Invite your team

    Add shops, branches, and partners. Data syncs peer-to-peer and stays private end to end.

Why bitos.space

Software your organization actually owns

Most enterprise tools keep your people, your money, and your data locked inside someone else's cloud. bitos.space flips that: it is a local-first enterprise social network built on Nostr, where your relay, your keys, and your records stay yours.

Run it for a single shop or a federation of branches — the same open protocol connects them without a central gatekeeper.

  • Local-first: works offline, syncs when connected
  • Your own keys and relay — zero vendor lock-in
  • Federated: connect shops, branches, and partners

Nostr native

Open protocol, real portability

Everything is built on public Nostr NIPs, so your data stays readable by any client — and keeps working even if this app disappears.

  • NIP-01

    Base protocol: events, relays, and subscriptions.

  • NIP-19

    Portable identities: npub and nprofile.

  • NIP-04 / 17

    Encrypted direct messages — legacy and private DMs.

  • NIP-57

    Lightning zaps, straight to your wallet.

  • NIP-25

    Reactions and lightweight social signals.

  • NIP-09

    Owner-controlled event deletion requests.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    The short version. Ask anything on GitHub for the long one.

    What exactly is bitos.space?

    A local-first enterprise social network built on Nostr. It brings chat, ERP, CRM, finance, and AI agents together and runs on infrastructure you own instead of a vendor's cloud.

    Do I need to run a server?

    No. Start on the hosted relay and demo app in seconds. When you are ready, self-host your own relay and point the same app at it — nothing else changes.

    Is my data private?

    Keys are generated and kept on your device, and messages are end-to-end encrypted. You choose which relay stores your data, so there is no central server reading it.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes. bitos.space is local-first: you keep working offline and everything syncs automatically when you reconnect.

    What does it cost?

    The software is open source and free to self-host, with no per-seat fees. You only pay for the infrastructure you choose to run.
